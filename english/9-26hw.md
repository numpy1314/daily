The app also offers a 'Daily English' section with tailored exercises based on my learning goals, which helps me stay consistent.

# What are the advantages and drawbacks of using AI in language learning?

- Advantages:
- Personalized Learning Experience: "AI tools excel at adapting to individual learning styles and pace. For example, when I struggle with a particular grammar point, Doubao will automatically generate more practice exercises focused on that area, rather than moving on to new material. This personalized approach ensures I master concepts before progressing."
- Instant, Detailed Feedback: "The speed of AI feedback is transformative. When I make a mistake in writing or pronunciation using Doubao, I receive correction immediately—often with explanations of why the error occurred and how to fix it. This contrasts with traditional learning, where I might wait days for a teacher's feedback."
- Flexibility and Accessibility: "AI tools eliminate time and location constraints. With Doubao on my phone, I can practice at 2 a.m. if I want, or squeeze in a quick session during my commute. This flexibility has made consistent practice much easier to maintain."
- Low-Stress Environment: "Speaking with AI reduces the anxiety many learners feel when practicing with native speakers. I can make mistakes without feeling self-conscious, which has helped me build confidence before engaging in real conversations."

- Drawbacks:
- Potential for Inaccuracies: "While generally reliable, AI tools aren't infallible. I've noticed Doubao occasionally provides explanations that are overly simplified or, in rare cases, incorrect—particularly with more complex grammatical structures or cultural nuances. This means I still need to verify information with other sources."
- Risk of Over-Reliance: "There's a danger of becoming too dependent on AI for writing or conversation. I've caught myself hesitating to write emails without Doubao's assistance, which makes me worry about my ability to produce accurate English independently in high-pressure situations."
